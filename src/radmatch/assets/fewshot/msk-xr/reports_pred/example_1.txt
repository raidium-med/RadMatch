EXAMINATION: RADIOGRAPHS OF THE LEFT HIP WITH AP PELVIS, 3 VIEWS

INDICATION: 82-year-old woman with left hip pain and inability to bear weight after a fall. Evaluate for fracture.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
No acute fracture. No hip dislocation. Moderate osteoarthritis of both hips with joint space narrowing and osteophytes. The bones are diffusely osteopenic. Bone island in the left femoral head.

IMPRESSION:
No acute fracture. If clinical suspicion for an occult fracture persists, MRI is recommended.
