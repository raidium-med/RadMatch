EXAMINATION: RADIOGRAPHS OF THE RIGHT HAND, 3 VIEWS

INDICATION: 24-year-old man with right hand pain and swelling after punching a wall.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
There is an acute fracture of the fourth metacarpal neck with approximately 35 degrees of volar angulation of the distal fragment. Normal alignment at the carpometacarpal and metacarpophalangeal joints. Radiopaque foreign body in the dorsal soft tissues over the fifth metacarpophalangeal joint. Normal bone mineralization.

IMPRESSION:
1. Acute right fourth metacarpal neck fracture with volar angulation.
2. Radiopaque foreign body over the fifth metacarpophalangeal joint. Clinical correlation for a penetrating injury is recommended.
