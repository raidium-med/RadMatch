EXAMINATION: RADIOGRAPHS OF THE LEFT HIP WITH AP PELVIS, 3 VIEWS

INDICATION: 82-year-old woman with left hip pain and inability to bear weight after a fall. Evaluate for fracture.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
There is an acute nondisplaced fracture of the left femoral neck. No hip dislocation. Mild osteoarthritis of both hips with joint space narrowing. The bones are diffusely osteopenic. Vascular calcifications.

IMPRESSION:
Acute nondisplaced left femoral neck fracture.
