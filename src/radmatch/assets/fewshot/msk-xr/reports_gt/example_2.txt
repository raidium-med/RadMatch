EXAMINATION: RADIOGRAPHS OF THE RIGHT HAND, 3 VIEWS

INDICATION: 24-year-old man with right hand pain and swelling after punching a wall.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
There is an acute fracture of the fifth metacarpal neck with approximately 35 degrees of volar angulation of the distal fragment. No dislocation. Normal bone mineralization.

IMPRESSION:
Acute right fifth metacarpal neck fracture with volar angulation.
