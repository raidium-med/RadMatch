EXAMINATION: ULTRASOUND ABDOMEN, RIGHT UPPER QUADRANT

INDICATION: 45-year-old woman with right upper quadrant pain and fever. Evaluate for cholecystitis.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
LIVER: Diffusely increased echogenicity, consistent with hepatic steatosis. No focal liver lesion. The main portal vein is patent with hepatofugal flow.
GALLBLADDER: Multiple shadowing gallstones. The gallbladder wall is thickened to 5 mm, with pericholecystic fluid. Positive sonographic Murphy sign.
BILE DUCTS: No intrahepatic biliary dilatation. The common bile duct is normal in caliber, measuring 5 mm.
RIGHT KIDNEY: Unremarkable.

IMPRESSION:
1. Cholelithiasis with gallbladder wall thickening, pericholecystic fluid and a positive sonographic Murphy sign, suspicious for acute cholecystitis.
2. Hepatofugal flow in the main portal vein.
