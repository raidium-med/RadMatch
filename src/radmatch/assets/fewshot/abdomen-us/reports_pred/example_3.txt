EXAMINATION: ULTRASOUND OF THE KIDNEYS AND BLADDER

INDICATION: 74-year-old man with acute kidney injury and difficulty voiding.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
KIDNEYS: No hydronephrosis. 2.4 cm complex cyst with a thick septation in the upper pole of the left kidney.
BLADDER: Distended, with diffuse wall thickening and trabeculation. 8 mm echogenic shadowing focus in the bladder lumen, consistent with a bladder stone. Post-void residual 650 ml.

IMPRESSION:
1. Large post-void residual. No hydronephrosis.
2. Bladder stone.
3. 2.4 cm complex left renal cyst. CT or MRI is recommended.
