EXAMINATION: ULTRASOUND OF THE KIDNEYS AND BLADDER

INDICATION: 74-year-old man with acute kidney injury and difficulty voiding.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
KIDNEYS: Moderate hydronephrosis of both kidneys. 2.4 cm complex cyst with a thick septation in the upper pole of the right kidney.
BLADDER: Distended, with diffuse wall thickening and trabeculation. Post-void residual 650 ml.
PROSTATE: Enlarged, with a volume of 62 ml.

IMPRESSION:
1. Moderate bilateral hydronephrosis and a large post-void residual.
2. Enlarged prostate.
3. 2.4 cm complex right renal cyst. CT or MRI is recommended.
