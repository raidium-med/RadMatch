EXAMINATION: ULTRASOUND ABDOMEN, RIGHT UPPER QUADRANT

INDICATION: 45-year-old woman with right upper quadrant pain and fever. Evaluate for cholecystitis.

COMPARISON: None.

FINDINGS:
LIVER: Diffusely increased echogenicity, compatible with hepatic steatosis. No focal liver lesion. The main portal vein is patent with hepatopetal flow, peak velocity 25 cm/s.
GALLBLADDER: Multiple shadowing gallstones. The gallbladder wall is thickened to 5 mm, with pericholecystic fluid. Positive sonographic Murphy sign.
BILE DUCTS: No intrahepatic biliary dilatation. The common bile duct measures 4 mm.
PANCREAS: The visualized pancreas is unremarkable. The pancreatic tail is obscured by bowel gas.
RIGHT KIDNEY: Unremarkable.

IMPRESSION:
Acute calculous cholecystitis.
