BRAIN MRI

Clinical information: Metastatic breast carcinoma, follow-up

Technique: Sagittal and axial T1-weighted; sagittal, axial and coronal FLAIR; axial T2-weighted, DWI, ADC and SWI. T1-weighted in 3 planes after contrast agent injection.

Comparison: [date_1] dated Cranial MRI

Findings:

- On the left inferior surface of the tentorium, a leptomeningeal metastasis that was of punctate size on the previous examination is now forming a mass 2 cm in diameter and 1.5 cm in thickness; massive edema extending to the brainstem and cerebellum associated with this mass is observed.
- Cerebellar metastases that enhanced on the previous examination have disappeared or transformed into cystic focal areas. No new metastatic foci added in the posterior fossa during the interval.
- Metastases measuring 17 mm in diameter in the right putamen, 5 mm in diameter in the head of the left caudate nucleus, and 1 cm in diameter in the left posterior parietal cortex have developed, not found on the previous examination. Extensive edema is present around the putaminal metastasis.
- Not found on the previous examination, an extra-axial metastasis in the left lateral occipital region, 17 mm in diameter, forming massive parenchymal edema, 5 mm in diameter within the left Sylvian fissure, and 3 extra-axial metastases in the left frontal have developed.
- The metastasis infiltrating the anterior lobe and stalk of the pituitary gland appears stable on the current examination.
- Bone metastases are present in the clivus and cervical vertebrae, and bilateral mandibular condyles, but bone metastasis is not seen in the cranium.

Impression:

- Parenchymal metastases in the putamen, caudate nucleus, and parietal lobe; multiple new leptomeningeal metastases in the left infratentorial region and left cerebral convexity.
- Pituitary metastasis appears stable.
- Widespread bone metastases.
